# PAPER SUITCASES — Wara Blue
## Global Diaspora Launch Campaign

**Positioning line (the one sentence the whole campaign sells):**
> *One bag. A whole life inside it. An Afrobeats anthem for everyone who left home to build one.*

**Who we're talking to:** the global diaspora — Africans abroad, immigrants, refugees,
first- and second-gen kids, anyone who's started over in a new country. The song isn't
about one family; it's about *every* family that ever packed a paper suitcase.

**Core emotional hooks to hammer (pick one per post, don't blur them):**
1. *Sacrifice* — what people give up to give their kids a chance.
2. *Hope* — the reason you get on the boat / plane / road at all.
3. *Belonging* — carrying "home" with you when you have no home.
4. *Pride* — the diaspora as survivors, not victims.
